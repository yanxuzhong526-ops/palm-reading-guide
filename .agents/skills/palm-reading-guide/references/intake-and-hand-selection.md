# Intake and hand selection

## Minimum photo set

Request both palms when possible. A usable photo should show the full palm from wrist crease to fingertips, face the camera directly, retain natural skin texture, and have enough resolution to inspect the three major lines. Ask the user to remove rings or bracelets only if they cover relevant areas.

Preferred capture:

- Camera parallel to palm, about 35–50 cm away.
- Soft daylight or diffuse indoor light; avoid flash glare and hard shadows.
- Neutral background with clear contrast.
- Fingers comfortably open, thumb relaxed, palm not stretched tight.
- One left-palm image and one right-palm image; optional close-ups for faint secondary lines.

Reject or downgrade confidence for filters, motion blur, excessive compression, cropped wrist/fingers, strong perspective, clenched hands, wet/oily skin, or deep shadow.

## Required profile fields

Ask for the following in one message when missing:

1. Gender profile: 男性 / 女性 / 非二元或其他 / 不透露.
2. Dominant hand: 左 / 右 / 双手均衡 / 不确定.
3. Mode: modern dominant-hand reading, or traditional 男左女右.
4. Both palm photos.

Do not ask for birth date, address, occupation, relationship status, health history, or other personal data unless the user voluntarily wants contextual reflection and it is relevant.

## Hand-selection matrix

| Profile | Modern mode (default) | Traditional-gendered mode |
|---|---|---|
| 男性 | Dominant hand primary; other hand comparative | Left hand primary, right comparative |
| 女性 | Dominant hand primary; other hand comparative | Right hand primary, left comparative |
| 非二元/其他 | Dominant hand primary; other hand comparative | Dominant hand primary; explain that the binary convention does not map cleanly |
| 不透露 | Dominant hand primary; other hand comparative | Dominant hand primary; do not guess gender |

The reading content is not biologically gendered. Gender changes only the selected traditional convention and, where the user prefers, address terms. Never produce stereotyped claims such as “男性事业更强” or “女性感情更细腻”.

## Confidence rubric

- **High**: feature is clearly visible across enough pixels, its start/end are traceable, and lighting does not obscure it.
- **Medium**: most of the feature is visible but one segment, junction, or depth judgment is uncertain.
- **Low**: only a fragment is visible, the photo is distorted, or competing creases make identification ambiguous.
- **Not assessable**: do not interpret; explain the image limitation and state what photo would help.
