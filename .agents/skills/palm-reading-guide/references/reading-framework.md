# Palm-reading framework

Use this as a consistency guide for traditional symbolism, not as scientific evidence. Always lead each section with the observed feature.

## Observation order

1. Overall hand: palm length-to-width, finger length, flexibility cues visible in the pose, skin/line density only when lighting is reliable.
2. Thumb and fingers: thumb opening, phalange proportions, finger spacing, relative finger lengths, fingertip shapes. Avoid value judgments.
3. Major lines: heart, head, life.
4. Secondary lines: fate, Sun/Apollo, Mercury/health, relationship, travel, intuition—only when clearly visible.
5. Mounts: Venus, Moon/Luna, Jupiter, Saturn, Apollo, Mercury, upper/lower Mars, plain of Mars.
6. Cross-hand comparison: location, depth, continuity, and new/absent branches. “Not visible” is not the same as “absent”.

## Major lines

### Heart line

Observe origin below the little finger, depth, curvature, chain/islands, breaks, branches, and endpoint below/between the index and middle fingers. Traditional themes: emotional expression, relational style, boundaries, idealism versus restraint. Do not predict marriage outcome, fidelity, sexuality, or psychiatric state.

### Head line

Observe whether it begins joined to or separate from the life line, length, slope, depth, forks, interruptions, and destination toward the lunar side. Traditional themes: decision style, concentration, practical versus imaginative orientation, adaptability. Do not equate line shape with intelligence, neurodivergence, or competence.

### Life line

Observe its start, arc around the Venus mount, depth, continuity, sister lines, branches, and intersections. Traditional themes: pace, resilience symbolism, attachment to familiar environments, and shifts in life rhythm. Explicitly state that length does not measure lifespan and breaks do not predict death or illness.

### Fate line

Observe presence, origin near wrist/life line/Moon, depth, interruptions, changes after crossing the head or heart line, and endpoint. Traditional themes: structure, vocation narrative, external versus self-directed influences, and changes in priorities. Absence is common and not negative. Do not predict exact career events or income.

## Secondary lines

- **Sun/Apollo line**: traditional symbolism of recognition, creative satisfaction, or visibility; not guaranteed fame.
- **Mercury/health line**: describe as a Mercury line and discuss communication or work rhythm only. Never diagnose health from it.
- **Relationship lines**: discuss as symbolic patterns of attachment or commitment reflection; never count marriages, children, divorces, or partners.
- **Travel/influence lines**: discuss openness to change or environmental shifts; do not promise relocation or dates.

## Mounts

Describe fullness relative to the same hand, not against an imagined universal norm.

| Mount | Location | Traditional themes |
|---|---|---|
| Venus | Base of thumb | warmth, vitality symbolism, attachment, sensory engagement |
| Moon/Luna | Outer lower palm | imagination, intuition symbolism, receptivity, movement |
| Jupiter | Below index finger | aspiration, initiative, leadership orientation |
| Saturn | Below middle finger | responsibility, reflection, caution |
| Apollo | Below ring finger | aesthetics, expression, visibility |
| Mercury | Below little finger | communication, exchange, practical agility |
| Mars zones | Inner/outer mid-palm | persistence, boundaries, response to pressure |

Avoid “good/bad” labels. Use balanced pairs such as expressive/reserved, steady/flexible, self-directed/context-responsive.

## Hand-shape archetypes

If the image supports the proportions, traditional elemental archetypes may be used as a secondary lens:

- Earth: broad/square palm with relatively shorter fingers; grounded, concrete, steady symbolism.
- Air: square/rectangular palm with longer fingers; analytical, communicative, mentally active symbolism.
- Fire: longer palm with shorter fingers; direct, energetic, momentum-oriented symbolism.
- Water: longer/narrower palm with longer fingers; receptive, imaginative, nuanced symbolism.

Mixed types are common. Never force a category or use it as a personality diagnosis.

## Gender branch

The male and female branches use the same evidence and interpretive vocabulary. The only default difference is hand selection in `traditional-gendered` mode. For non-binary, other, or undisclosed profiles, stay with dominant/non-dominant hand analysis. If the user asks for gender-specific framing, personalize examples and forms of address without assigning gendered traits.

## Synthesis rules

- Require at least two independent visible cues before naming a central theme.
- Pair each strength with a possible tension, phrased non-fatally.
- Prefer present-tense reflection over future prediction.
- Mention contradictions rather than smoothing them away; palms often show mixed symbolic cues.
- Label the confidence of every line and every synthesis claim.
